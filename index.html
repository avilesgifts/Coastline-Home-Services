FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do you operate?

South Surrey, White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley City and Langley Township. Each network covers a set area so partners aren't driving across the Lower Mainland.

What does "one partner per trade" mean?

Each network has exactly one plumber, one electrician, one roofer, and so on. When a partner hears about work in your trade, it comes to you, not to a competitor.

Do I pay per referral?

No. Membership is a flat monthly fee. Whether you get five referrals or fifty, the price stays the same.

How much time does it take?

One 60-minute partner meetup a month, plus a few seconds to send a referral from your phone. We handle recruiting, tracking, reminders and follow-up.

Do I need the Smart System to join the network?

No, but it helps. Referrals convert best when the homeowner hears back within minutes, and the Smart System makes sure that always happens.

Is there a contract?

Memberships are month-to-month after a 90-day founding period, which gives the network time to build momentum.

Is your trade still open in your area?

Seats are limited to one business per trade. Book a free 20-minute call and we'll tell you where things stand.
